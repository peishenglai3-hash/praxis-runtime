## ContextPlanner decision

The audit confirms that `ContextPlanner.plan()` consumes `taskRelevance`, `projectRelevance`, `recency`, `explicitPriority`, and `activeRuleRelevance` as inputs and performs weighted ranking, budget selection, and exposure planning. The evaluator derives those factors; the planner does not infer them from raw task meaning.

The freeze-safe interpretation is therefore:

> `ContextPlanner` is a bounded planner/ranker over supplied relevance signals.

It is not a learned relevance engine and no learned subsystem was added. A future internal-judgement mechanism would require a new architecture decision and evidence plan.

## Asset decision

The evaluator now distinguishes `REFLECTION` from `FULL PRAXIS` in executable behavior. `FULL PRAXIS` can record a residual, propose a candidate, validate it after independent evidence, ask an explicit human-control hook, activate only after confirmation, and later expose, ignore, or challenge the active asset. No hook means no promotion. Direct candidate-to-active promotion is rejected by the runtime contract.

This is **harness-level end-to-end evidence**, not proof that an asset improves a real task. The latter remains an empirical gate.

- **Implemented but only partially evidenced:** real external verification, cross-agent cursor behavior, lagging-valid projection detection, full doctor §13.1 coverage, FieldContext validity, and provider portability.
